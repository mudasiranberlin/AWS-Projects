# Billing and Cost Management Home

## Overview

The Billing and Cost Management Home provides a centralized view of AWS spending, billing information, and cost management tools. It enables administrators and finance teams to monitor current charges, review usage trends, manage payment settings, and optimize cloud costs.

### Purpose

Use the Billing and Cost Management dashboard to:

* Monitor AWS account spending and usage
* Review invoices and payment history
* Track cost trends and forecasts
* Analyze service-level costs
* Manage budgets and cost controls
* Access cost optimization recommendations

This dashboard serves as the primary location for financial visibility and cost governance across the AWS environment.
